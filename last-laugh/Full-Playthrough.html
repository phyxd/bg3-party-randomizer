The Last Laugh

Dark Urge · four seats · Honour · PS5 · level 20 · AMP

The premise

  • You are the charming problem everyone should have killed in Act 1. The party wins through control, ranged focus fire, unarmed disruption and a recovery plan.
  • This is a curated test playthrough, not a claim that a blind random roll found the strongest possible party.
  • Default story: evil Durge, retaining Minthara, Shadowheart and Lae'zel. This carries forward the active run's preference. Story spoilers appear below.
  • Light Cleric is Shadowheart's combat respec; it does not force a deity or story outcome. Race and background remain fixed for companions.
  • Four new catalog variants: N1–N4. They are rules-checked, not yet playtested in your installed PS5 setup.

Launch setup

  1. Start a new campaign. Keep Bhaal Till You Fall and its exported JSON intact.
  2. Create a Half-Drow Dark Urge, Bard. Use the exact Durge creation spread and skill choices in the character chapter. Appearance and name are yours.
  3. Freeze and record the enabled PS5 mod list and versions. Required: Patch 8, total-level-20 extension and AMP. The builds need no added class pack. If an existing mod overrides a core class, test that override before relying on these paths.
  4. Keep Blood Mage, Pactbound, cheat Slayer gear and unlimited-Strength-elixir assumptions out of this profile. Do not uninstall dependencies from an existing save to imitate it.
  5. Start AMP at the preferred Casual setting with merchant takeover disabled, carried forward from Run 2. Add difficulty only after seeing real combat results. No simultaneous jump in enemy actions, initiative rules and encounter count.
  6. Use the Training Ring or a disposable test save to inspect all four class paths through total level 20, attack counts, spell slots and item interactions. A passing JavaScript test cannot establish PS5 mod compatibility.
  7. In the new companion, record observed scores after creation bonuses. The initial displayed spread is the 27-point base, not your final character sheet.

Act 1: build the engine before taking the hard fights

  1. On the Nautiloid, prioritize reaching the helm alive. Optional boss loot is not a build requirement, especially with randomized rewards.
  2. Recruit Shadowheart, Lae'zel and Astarion. Reach Withers and assign the intended classes when possible. Gather ordinary armour, a finesse weapon/shield for Durge, a bow for the ranged seat and clothing for the Monk.
  3. Explore the Grove and surrounding accessible areas before committing to its outcome. Do available trading and side content first. The evil route deliberately loses some allies, vendors and quests; the guide does not budget their later rewards.
  4. Resolve the goblin/grove plot in Minthara's favour for this default route. Keep her alive. This is irreversible story commitment, not an optimization trick.
  5. Explore the Blighted Village and safer wilderness encounters; approach gnolls, the swamp/hag and gith patrol only after checking resources and escape routes. Avoid treating the XP path as a fixed level timetable because the extension and XP mods change it.
  6. Complete desired Underdark content and assess Grymforge separately. The forge boss is optional risk; no fixed mould or named item is required for this party.
  7. Before the Mountain Pass transition, finish the Act 1 quests you intend to retain. Read the in-game transition warning. Explore the monastery/crèche when the party has its core attacks and control, keeping Lae'zel's story consequences deliberate.
  8. Review gear and record one lesson before entering the Shadow-Cursed Lands. Finish accessible Act 1 tasks before the later Shadowfell lockout.

Act 1 combat check

  • Ranged seat reaches Fighter 5 for two attacks; Monk reaches Monk 5 for two attacks and stun attempts. Durge reaches Bard 6 for Extra Attack. Shadowheart reaches Cleric 5 for Spirit Guardians.
  • Do not dip early just to resemble the endpoint. These core unlocks matter more than collecting class names.
  • No surprise round, permanent advantage or legendary drop is assumed. If a control spell fails, move to focus fire and recovery rather than spending the whole party repeating it.

Act 2: secure the roster before closing the map

  1. Obtain protection appropriate to the current shadow-curse depth. Ordinary light is not a promise of protection everywhere; inspect conditions before travelling.
  2. Visit Moonrise while you can still interact rather than treating it as a final-dungeon-only location. Rescue and recruit Minthara, then transfer the ranged seat.
  3. Complete desired tower, prison and regional business before entering the Shadowfell. That transition cuts off Act 1 and advances unfinished content. See Shadowfell consequences.
  4. Explore Reithwin's encounters and the Gauntlet in an order your current resources support. Dialogue is a tool, not an assumption that every dangerous encounter can be bypassed.
  5. Decide Shadowheart's Nightsong outcome explicitly. An evil route can accept severe ally/content losses; the Light-domain respec does not protect anyone from story consequences. Do not promise a full allied endgame roster after sacrificing it.
  6. Before the Moonrise finale, prepare recovery, mobility and non-concentration damage. Choose a concentration job for each caster. Refill short-rest resources and confirm actual consumables.
  7. For Ketheric/Myrkul, inspect the current encounter's mechanics and legendary actions. Keep recovery and ranged pressure available, clear dangerous supporting enemies, and avoid clumping everyone where movement or healing is constrained.

Act 3: finish deliberately

  1. Establish access through Rivington and the city, review the journal and identify the required Netherstone path. Do not start every optional boss chain at once.
  2. Resolve companion commitments deliberately: Lae'zel's loyalty and the Orpheus/Emperor decision, Shadowheart's remaining story, and Durge's confrontation with Orin. They affect the ending more than a small item-score upgrade.
  3. Before committing to Orin, prepare Durge to function without assuming the other three can immediately solve the encounter. Confirm dialogue/duel conditions, immunity, escape options and usable resources for the chosen story branch.
  4. Treat Gortash, the Foundry and associated rescue consequences as a linked decision. Check active quest objectives before hostile actions; there is no universal safe order after every possible alliance or rescue choice.
  5. Optional fights such as Raphael or Ansur are content goals, not mandatory gear shopping. Attempt them after a dedicated boss brief and a clear reason to accept the Honour risk. Randomized loot invalidates guaranteed named-item shopping lists.
  6. Before the Morphic Pool, complete desired side quests, allocate every important item, spend planned crafting materials, respec if necessary, rest and carry what you need. This is a point of no return with no normal return to camp. See Morphic Pool.
  7. In the finale, prioritize objectives and route safety over killing everything. Use allies you actually retained. Save burst for the phases that need it and follow current encounter tooltips for immunities and platform hazards.
  8. Choose the final Durge/Netherbrain outcome as a story decision. The party's mechanics do not require taking a specific ending.

One-screen encounter plan

  1. Threat: which enemy action must not happen next?
  2. Control: what is susceptible, and which caster owns concentration?
  3. Kill: who finishes that target, and does the order actually work at this initiative?
  4. Recovery: who can stand up an ally without losing the whole plan?
  5. Exit: what is the retreat or recovery route if the first control attempt fails?

AMP priorities and budget

  • Keep a usable kit on all four seats before spending everything on one legendary branch.
  • First comparison: gain over the item being replaced. Then check eligibility, lost passives, concentration and the best new owner for displaced gear.
  • Durge wants finesse accuracy and caster utility; Minthara wants ranged accuracy; Shadowheart wants concentration and support; Lae'zel wants unarmed/DEX/WIS benefits. All four do not automatically want the same crit shard.
  • The workshop covers the documented enchanting ladder and material tier conversions. Orbs and bespoke shard effects remain tooltip-verification decisions; no unverified capsule recipes are fabricated.
  • Track one exact target batch at a time. Material totals and completed crafts are manual records, never automatically read from PS5.

Session rhythm and difficulty calibration

  • Start: read the next objective, confirm current levels, review unassigned loot and choose a concentration plan.
  • After a difficult encounter: record control failures, damage type problems, concentration losses and resources used. One concise observation is enough.
  • End: export a backup, write the next objective and record unresolved tooltips.
  • After three representative encounters, adjust one difficulty setting if needed. A party that wins through consumable depletion and repeated downing is not ready for extra enemy actions merely because it survived.

Launch acceptance checklist

  • Four creation spreads cost 27; creation bonuses and every ASI match the intended observed scores.
  • All 80 leveling rows are executable in the installed profile; no class exceeds 12.
  • Fighter 11 allows three attacks per Attack action; Haste is not assumed to duplicate full Honour-mode attack chains.
  • Monk control and chosen Fighter manoeuvres behave as described; weapon-only manoeuvres are not counted on punches.
  • The total-level extension's multiclass slots load correctly and do not grant unlearned spells.
  • AMP recipe/version checks pass for the actual target. No armour chest ladder, guaranteed randomized drop or invented shard is required.
  • Astarion-to-Minthara handover and the chosen evil-route losses are understood.

What this package is

  • A complete campaign framework, 80-row character progression, compact combat plan and separate playable companion.
  • It is not an exhaustive location-by-location completionist map, nor a claim of in-game validation. The launch checklist is the next test, not a formality.

The Last Laugh - party engine

The win condition

  1. Durge or Shadowheart disables or contains the dangerous group.
  2. Minthara removes the priority threat from range.
  3. Lae'zel disrupts the enemy that could still break the plan.
  4. Shadowheart preserves the party's ability to take another turn.

Concentration ownership

  • Durge: Hypnotic Pattern, Hold or Hunger of Hadar; choose one.
  • Shadowheart: Bless, Spirit Guardians or terrain; choose one.
  • Minthara: optional Hunter's Mark only when it does not crowd out a better bonus action.
  • Lae'zel: no concentration dependency.

Initiative and resource tradeoffs

  • If the archer acts before control lands, shoot another dangerous target rather than pretending the planned combo already exists.
  • Prone can hinder distant ranged attacks; select Stagger or another target when appropriate.
  • Two short rests plus Song of Rest support Bard Inspiration, ki and Fighter resources. They do not replenish everything a long rest does.
  • Durge has no Paladin 6 aura in this build. Defence comes from positioning, reactions and support, not an aura the split never reaches.
  • Shadowheart's Starry Dragon form improves concentration rolls; large damage can still demand a roll it cannot guarantee.

Opening round

  1. Inspect the priority target.
  2. Pick one control plan, one damage target and one recovery owner.
  3. Commit burst only when it changes the next enemy turn.
  4. Keep a route out. If the opening fails, stabilize rather than chain every resource into the same bad assumption.

The signature loop (make the next enemy turn worse)

  1. Check target saves and immunity before choosing control or damage.
  2. Control a dangerous group with Hypnotic Pattern, or isolate a lane with Hunger of Hadar. Keep allies outside it.
  3. On a disabled priority target, move into eligible melee range and use weapon attacks with Ask-mode smites.
  4. Use Counterspell for a spell that threatens the fight; it competes with Shield for your reaction.

How it plays

The signature loop (make the next enemy turn worse)

  1. Identify the enemy whose next turn is most dangerous and reach it without stranding yourself.
  2. Use Stagger to remove reactions or Topple when it helps close-range allies; distant archers can dislike prone targets.
  3. Spend Stunning Strike selectively, then Flurry to finish or control. Do not exhaust ki merely because two bonus actions are available.
  4. Retain movement or a bonus action for escape. Short-rest ki recovery and Song of Rest support the next encounter.

How it plays

  • Act 1: secure the primary class engine before dipping; prefer manageable encounters and exploration to early boss gambles.
  • Act 2: keep your concentration assignment and escape route explicit; compare actual upgrades after each reward.
  • Act 3: complete the remaining levels when earned, use a compact boss plan, and stop treating every optional encounter as mandatory.

  • Clothing, no shield, and unarmed-friendly bonuses. DEX/WIS, accuracy, movement and defensive saves matter. Do not choose Tavern Brawler on this STR 8 plan. A Strength-based variant would need its own sustainable ability plan, not assumed infinite elixirs.
  • Orb theme to inspect: Agate (War); select the actual branch by tooltip, not name alone.
