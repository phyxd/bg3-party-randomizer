  1. One save file. Party wipe = the run ends (or you continue in Custom mode with Honour lost).
  2. Legendary Actions on ~30 boss fights: bosses act at the end of other creatures' turns. Plan for the boss to punish you between turns.
  3. Extra Attack never stacks across classes (any difficulty). In Honour, Pact of the Blade also does not stack with Extra Attack, and the extra action from Haste / Bloodlust does not get Extra Attack. Action Surge still does.

Prescribed starting config (a 4-seat party is the mod's baseline, so party-size scaling barely matters here):

  1. Enemy extra HP: modest (the lowest non-zero step the menu offers). AMP already makes Act 2 brutal for four; start gentle.
  2. Enemy extra actions: OFF.
  3. Enemy extra bonus actions: OFF.
  4. Party-size scaling: ON if offered (it does nothing at 4 seats but future-proofs the save if you ever add a fifth).
  5. Extra Encounters and Minibosses: ON from the start (it is content, not a stat knob).

Escalation ladder (turn ONE knob per step; play at least one act between steps):

  1. Raise enemy HP one step.
  2. Enemy bonus actions +1.
  3. Enemy actions +1 (this is the big one; Honour bosses already have Legendary Actions — expect double-turn spikes).
  4. Only after that: consider "More enemies in basic fights" (Mod KB flags not to stack it on a first run).

⚙️ THE PARTY ENGINE — how these four actually work together

Party S3, rolled 2026-08-15 by the synergy sampler (400 candidate parties scored, top tier rolled). This is the one-page answer to "what do I press."

The win condition: the Paralysis Cashier

One sentence: Shadowheart takes away the target's turn, and two smiters plus a crit-fisher cash it in the same round.

  1. A Paralysed creature (Hold Person / Hold Monster) takes critical hits from attacks that hit within 3 m (the attack roll still matters), and auto-fails STR and DEX saves.
  2. Durge Action Surges into it: up to 4 attacks from character 15 when Action Surge is available; qualifying hits are critical, each carrying Divine Smite + Radiant Strikes, every die doubled, plus +1d4 per hit if Enlarge is up (Duergar, 10 turns, no concentration).
  3. Lae'zel curses it first (crit on 19–20, proficiency to damage) and Action Surges for up to 4 attacks before Fighter 11 or 6 attacks from Fighter 11 (character 15); qualifying hits are critical.
  4. Minthara adds Conquest mark advantage and her own smites, or holds Counterspell if the boss is a caster.
  5. A Paralysed target rolls a save at the end of each of its turns, so the window is one round. Everything above must happen in that round — call the target out loud.

Three support layers

  • LAYER 1 — Aura coverage (survive). Activate Aura of Protection and check its icon/radius on the character sheet. Two paladins provide overlapping coverage and redundancy; do not add two CHA modifiers into the plan without a combat-log check of the installed rules. Stay in range when useful, but spread against area damage rather than blindly clustering.
  • LAYER 2 — Fear and lockdown (deny). Durge's Dreadful Aspect frightens a room, Spiteful Suffering taxes the boss, Abjure Foe (PHB2024 mod) strips its action economy, Shadowheart's Hypnotic Pattern removes the adds. This party wins by making fewer enemies act, not by killing faster.
  • LAYER 3 — Check resistances (kill). Inspect the actual enemy before choosing physical, radiant or psychic damage; none is universally unresisted. ⚠ AMP undead and fiends resist necrotic and the AI dodges resisted damage — which is why nobody in this party leans on necrotic despite the theme. Durge's necrotic Spiteful Suffering is a debuff, not a kill.

Turn rotation (late-game template; check current levels and resources)

  1. Shadowheart (if her actual initiative permits; Alert is not taken until Wizard 16 on this path): Hold Monster on the boss, or Hypnotic Pattern if it is a crowd. Announce the target.
  2. Lae'zel: Hexblade's Curse (bonus action) on that target, then the Attack action (2 attacks, or 3 from Fighter 11), then Action Surge for another Attack action if available and worthwhile.
  3. Durge: walk in so both auras cover the party, choose Dreadful Aspect OR Attack + smite for the action. An additional action needs an available resource such as Action Surge, which arrives at character 15 on this path.
  4. Minthara: the Conquest mark and smite the same target, or Quickened Haste on Lae'zel first, or Counterspell held for the boss.
  5. Everyone: stay inside the aura radius. Leaving it is how someone fails a save and the run ends.

Honour-mode panic buttons

  1. Durge's Lay on Hands is a bonus action (PHB2024 mod) — spend the bonus action and healing resource; verify the mod is installed, otherwise use the base-game action cost.
  2. Durge's Revivify (Paladin 9) is the run's insurance. Keep a slot for it at all times in Act 2 and 3.
  3. Durge's Duergar Invisibility (character 5+) — free, once per combat, no slot: the disengage when he is about to drop. It breaks the instant he attacks, so it is an escape, not a tactic. (Note: swapping Half-Orc out means the party no longer has Relentless Endurance — nobody gets a free survival at 0 HP any more. Play the front line more carefully.)
  4. Minthara's Strength of the Grave — a chance to survive at 1 HP instead of dropping.
  5. Counterspell (once learned, with a slot and reaction available) should stop the most consequential hostile spell; use judgment rather than restricting it to boss labels. Legendary Actions mean bosses act between your turns; assume they get one more cast than you expect.

What this party is bad at, and what to do about it

  • No dedicated healer. Two paladins with Lay on Hands and one Revivify is the whole kit. Buy every healing potion you see and keep Withers' revival cost in mind.
  • No stealth or trap specialist. Nobody has Expertise in Perception. Move slowly in Act 2; take Perception proficiency on Shadowheart wherever the level-up offers it.
  • Concentration collisions. Minthara wants Haste, Shadowheart wants Hold Monster, and both want Darkness or Hypnotic Pattern. Default: Shadowheart holds the lock; Minthara selects one concentration spell based on the encounter. Casting Darkness, Haste, Shadow Blade or another concentration spell replaces her current concentration; she cannot maintain all of them.
  • Two seats need the same crit shards. Durge and Lae'zel both want crit-threshold and Critical strikes. Lae'zel gets first pick (she has the 19–20 window to widen); Durge takes the second set.

Difficulty dial (from the run charter §5b)

  • AMP runs on Casual (unlocked chests, merchant restock on rest, Pyre off) with merchant takeover disabled, so the AMP side of the difficulty is soft and gold buys gear.
  • Start: Honour + Tactician Enhanced with enemy HP two steps up and enemy bonus actions +1, enemy actions OFF, Extra Encounters and Minibosses ON.
  • Escalate one knob at a time: HP up → enemy actions +1. Bosses already have Legendary Actions; enemy actions is the real cliff.
  • Shopping is now part of the loop: merchants restock on long rest, so buy before resting, and never blind-sell uniques (they are disenchant dust).

💀 DURGE — "Fear Itself" · Role: The Tyrant (fear-lock anchor + smite cashier)

Oathbreaker Paladin 13 / Battle Master Fighter 7

The Urge, embraced and given a command voice. He does not sneak, he does not negotiate: he walks into the middle of the room, makes everything too afraid to fight back, and cashes the ones that stay.

The pitch:

  • He is the party's only real anchor. Heavy armour + shield, Aura of Protection (+CHA to every save in the huddle) and Aura of Courage covering three squishier seats. In honour mode, one failed save is how runs end; he is the answer to that.
  • Fear is the control layer. Dreadful Aspect frightens a room, Spiteful Suffering pins one target with damage-per-turn plus advantage for him, Bestow Curse (or Abjure Foe from the PHB2024 mod) takes a boss's turn apart.
  • Aura of Hate is the payoff nobody else in this party can use. +CHA modifier to melee damage for himself and any nearby Undead or Fiends. With Better Oathbreaker Summons installed, his own summons at Paladin 7 and 12 are Undead, but their melee weapon attacks must be verified before counting the aura bonus. Nearby enemy undead/fiends can also benefit.
  • Duergar gives him a free damage buff and an escape hatch. Enlarge (+1d4 weapon damage, advantage on STR checks and saves, 10 turns, no concentration, once per long rest) adds damage on eligible hits; the comparison with a crit die depends on the fight, and Invisibility (free, no slot, once per combat and at-will outside it) is the honour-mode disengage nobody else in this party has.
  • Why 13/7 and not pure Paladin 20? Fighter's tail buys Action Surge (a second full smite action on the turn that matters), a Battle Master control kit, and two extra feats. Paladin 14–20 buys slow half-caster slots he does not need.

Mods: base game (Patch 8) + UnlockLevelCurve + Paladin and Oaths DnD 5R PHB2024 + optional Better Oathbreaker Summons. · PS5-safe. ⚠ Character levels 13+ ride UnlockLevelCurve — the Fighter features are base-game, only the level unlock is modded; verify each at the Training Ring. ⚠ Confirm both paladin mods carry a Console tag in the in-game browser before relying on them; the build works on base Oathbreaker without either.

Race · Deity · Background

  • Race: Duergar (Gray Dwarf) — Tyler's override 2026-08-16, replacing the rolled Half-Orc. Traits, all verified on bg3.wiki:
  • Superior Darkvision 24m (double a normal dwarf's).
  • Duergar Resilience — advantage on saving throws against illusions, charm and paralysis; plus Dwarven Resilience (advantage vs poison, poison resistance).
  • Duergar Magic — Enlarge at level 3: +1d4 weapon damage, advantage on STR checks and saves, one size larger, 10 turns, no concentration, once per long rest, no spell slot.
  • Duergar Magic — Invisibility at level 5: free, once per combat and unlimited outside combat, no slot.
  • Dwarven Combat Training (battleaxe, handaxe, light hammer, warhammer), speed 7.5m.
  • Racial ability bonuses are fully floating in Patch 8, so CHA +2 / CON +1 is legal on a Duergar — the stat line below does not change.
  • ⚠ Read this before you count on the invisibility: the "busted" Duergar invisibility is a myth. BG3's Duergar Invisibility ends the moment you attack, cast a spell, interact with an object, take damage, or get wet — the same break conditions as the normal spell (bg3.wiki: Invisibility (Duergar)). What is genuinely strong is that it costs nothing and is reusable, so it is a scouting and escape tool, not an attack-while-invisible exploit. Do not build a turn around it.
  • What the swap costs: Half-Orc's Savage Attacks (an extra weapon die on every melee crit) and Relentless Endurance (drop to 1 HP instead of 0, once per long rest). Honest trade: Enlarge adds damage to eligible hits while active; its value versus Savage Attacks depends on hit count and critical-hit frequency, and the save advantages help in a party built around paralysis — but you are giving up a free "do not die" and the party's only such effect. Compensate by keeping Lay on Hands (bonus action) charged and never tanking on purpose.
  • Deity: flavour only — pick whatever the screen offers (Bhaal, obviously, in your head).
  • Background: Haunted One is the Durge default (Medicine + Intimidation in base BG3). Take whatever the origin locks; his skills do not carry the build.

Stats (point buy 27): STR 15 · DEX 10 · CON 14 · INT 8 · WIS 8 · CHA 15 (+2 CHA / +1 CON racial → CHA 17 · CON 15)

  • CHA 17 → 20 — the whole seat. Aura of Protection value, Aura of Hate damage, smite spell DCs, Dreadful Aspect DC. The default path reaches 20 without a story reward. Record any permanent bonus before adjusting the plan.
  • STR 15 → 19/20 — his to-hit and weapon damage. The elixir rule is OFF this run, so STR is real points and real ASIs. Do not dump it.
  • CON 15 — HP and concentration. He holds nothing critical, but honour mode punishes low HP.
  • DEX 10 / INT 8 / WIS 8 — dumps. Heavy armour makes DEX irrelevant to AC; his own Aura of Protection patches the bad saves for everyone including himself.

Eldritch Knight Fighter 16 / Hexblade Warlock 4

Gith discipline plus a patron she would never admit to having. She marks one target with a curse, and from that moment every 19 she rolls against it is a critical hit — in a party that hands her paralysed targets on a plate.

The pitch:

  • Hexblade's Curse is the crit engine. Against the cursed target she adds her proficiency bonus to damage, scores critical hits on a roll of 19 or 20, and regains HP when it dies. Curse the boss, then use the attacks unlocked at her current Fighter level: two per Attack action at Fighter 5, three at Fighter 11.
  • Action Surge doubles the best round. Fighter 2 gives a second full action; on a turn where Shadowheart has Paralysed the target, eligible melee hits automatically crit; two Attack actions allow four attacks at Fighter 5 and six at Fighter 11, provided the attacks hit and the target remains paralysed.
  • Eldritch Knight buys the utility a fighter normally begs for: Shield (reaction, +5 AC), Misty Step, and War Magic at Fighter 7 (cast a cantrip, then attack as a bonus action).
  • INT 8 is deliberate, not a mistake. She only casts spells that never roll a save: Shield, Misty Step, and (optionally) Shadow Blade. No INT-based DC is ever used, so the dump costs nothing. (Verified: this works precisely because none of those spells key off her spell save DC.)
  • Why 16/4 and not pure Fighter 20? The four warlock levels buy Hexblade's Curse, Pact of the Blade and a feat, and they cost her only the Fighter 17–20 tail. Extra Attack does not stack across classes. Fighter 11 separately grants Improved Extra Attack, for three attacks per Attack action. This is not a second class stacking Extra Attack.
